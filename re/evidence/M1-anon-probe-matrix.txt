--- READING NOTE (important, avoids a false-positive anomaly) ---
Every captured header dump starts with:
    HTTP/1.1 200 OK
    date: ...
This is the EGRESS PROXY's CONNECT tunnel handshake response (proxy at 127.0.0.1:18080), NOT an origin response.
The real origin response is the subsequent "HTTP/2 <status>" block. Do not read the leading 200 as part of the target's behaviour.
