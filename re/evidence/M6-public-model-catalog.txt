5. TYPOGRAPHIC NOTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The trailing/leading characters in `Phana-5.5o`, `Phana-5.5s`, `Phano-6a`, `Phano-6.1s` are easy to
mistype. Byte-level check of the HTML: `Phana-5.5o` ends with U+006F (Latin small letter o), and
`Phano-6a` / `Phano-6.1s` contain U+006F inside 'Phano'. No digit zero (U+0030) occurs in any display
name except `deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` ('1017'). Names are reproduced unchanged above; do not 'correct' them.
